# Privacy Policy for Passage

**Effective Date:** 5 September 2026
**Last Updated:** 16 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **HAPPY CHEEKS LTD** (company number 15146182), trading through the **Passage** mobile application ("Passage", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Passage mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App").

Passage is a soundscape app for sleep, relaxation and focus. It offers a catalogue of soundscapes, playlists, a sleep timer, and preferences that shape what is recommended to you.

**Passage is free to use.** There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, no trial period and no premium tier. We never ask you for payment details, we do not use a payment processor, and we hold no billing or payment information of any kind.

**Passage supports rest, relaxation and focus. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and it is not a medical device.** If you feel unsafe or are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis support line.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all users of the Passage App and to the information we collect through the App and its backend API. It does not apply to third-party services or platforms you connect to Passage (such as your Google account), which are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 3. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run the App for you.

### 3.1 Account information

- **Sign-in details:** your name and email address, and a securely stored password. Authentication is handled by our provider, **Supabase**; we never see your password in readable form.
- **Google sign-in (optional):** if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information from your Google account, such as your name and email address.
- **Profile picture (optional):** if you add one, the image file is stored in our Supabase Storage bucket and the App records its URL against your account.

### 3.2 Your content and activity in the App

- **Favourites** — the soundscapes you save.
- **Playlists** — the playlists you create and the soundscapes in them.
- **Play history and player state** — what you have played, when, and where you paused, so you can pick up where you left off.
- **Sound preferences** — preferred environments and sounds, sounds you have asked us to avoid, detail preference, and typical session length.
- **Playback and notification settings** — including your chosen wind-down reminder time, so your settings follow you to a new device.
- **Onboarding answers** — your goals, starting mode, and situation, if you provide them during setup.

### 3.3 Push notification tokens

All notifications are **off by default**. If, and only if, you turn a notification on, the App registers this device's push token with us so a notification can reach it. We keep a log of the notifications we send you. Turning notifications off, signing out, or deleting your account ends this.

### 3.4 Support requests

When you contact support, report a problem with a soundscape, or request a soundscape, we store your message, the subject or reason where you provide one, the soundscape concerned where relevant, and — if you are not signed in — the email address you give us so we can reply.

### 3.5 Usage analytics (optional, off by default)

Passage does **not** collect usage analytics unless you switch them on. The setting is off by default and lives in the App under Privacy & Data.

If you turn it on, we record anonymous product events — such as which features and soundscapes are used — to help us improve the catalogue and the App. Turning it off stops collection immediately, and our servers reject analytics events for accounts where the setting is off. We never record the content of your support messages, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Passage uses no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

### 3.6 Device and technical data

We process the limited technical information needed to run and troubleshoot the App and its API, such as the device platform associated with a push token, and standard server request logs.

### 3.7 What we do not collect

We do **not** collect health or fitness data, location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment information, and Passage does not connect to any health data platform.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- provide the App and play soundscapes, and let you resume where you left off;
- keep your favourites, playlists and preferences available and in sync when you sign in on another device;
- choose what to recommend to you, and hide soundscapes containing sounds you have asked to avoid;
- deliver the notifications you have turned on;
- answer your support requests and act on the problems you report;
- improve the App and its catalogue, using anonymous usage analytics **only if you have turned them on**;
- maintain, secure and troubleshoot the App;
- comply with our legal obligations.

We do not use your information to diagnose, treat, or make medical claims, and we do not sell it.

## 5. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR:

- **Performance of a contract** — to provide the App and its features to you. Passage is free, so this contract involves no payment.
- **Consent** — for optional notifications and for optional usage analytics. You may withdraw either at any time in the App.
- **Legitimate interests** — to maintain, secure and improve the App, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — where processing is required by law.

## 6. How We Share Your Information

**We do not sell your personal information.** We share it only in the limited circumstances below:

- **Service providers:** **Supabase**, which provides our authentication, database and file storage (including your profile picture), and our cloud backend infrastructure, both processing information on our behalf.
- **Push delivery:** **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Google), used to deliver push notifications — only if you have turned a notification on, and only for as long as it is on.
- **Sign-in:** **Google**, if you choose to sign in with your Google account.
- **Legal and safety reasons:** where required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public, or Passage.
- **Business transfers:** in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, where your information may transfer subject to this policy.
- **With your consent:** in any other case, with your explicit permission.

**There are no payment processors in this list, because Passage is free and processes no payments.**

## 7. Third-Party Services

Passage relies on the following third-party services:

- **Supabase** — authentication, database, and file storage.
- **Google** — sign-in (OAuth), if you use it.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — push notification delivery, if you enable notifications.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you connect.

> **Note on audio:** Passage's soundscapes are produced in advance and stored in our library, then streamed to your device. Your personal information is never sent to any audio-generation service to create them.

## 8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including:

- encryption in transit using HTTPS/TLS between the App and our production services;
- encryption at rest provided by Supabase for data held in the Supabase project;
- access controls limiting who can reach personal data;
- secure cloud storage with reputable providers.

Please note that profile pictures are stored in a **publicly readable** storage bucket, so anyone holding the image's URL can view it. Do not use an image you would not be comfortable sharing.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it, unless we are required to retain it for legal reasons.

When you delete your account, we remove your account record and authentication account, your favourites, play history, playlists, player state, sound, playback and notification settings, onboarding answers, registered device tokens, and your profile picture files.

Support requests, and any analytics events collected while you had analytics switched on, are **retained but unlinked from your account** — the link to you is removed so the remaining record is anonymous. Information may also persist briefly in service-provider backups and logs until those records age out under the provider's normal retention cycle.

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your information. Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- **access** the personal information we hold about you;
- **correct** inaccurate information — you can update your name, email, password and profile picture in the App under Account;
- **export your data** — the App offers "Export my data" under Privacy & Data, which produces a file containing everything we hold for you;
- **delete your account and data** — available in the App under Account, and under Privacy & Data, subject to the retention limits described in Section 9;
- **withdraw consent** for notifications or usage analytics at any time in the App;
- **object to or restrict** certain processing;
- **lodge a complaint** with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right not available directly in the App, contact us at **info@halcyonapp.io**. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
