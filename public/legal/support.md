# Passage Support

**Passage** is a soundscape app for sleep, relaxation and focus, from **HAPPY CHEEKS LTD**. If you need help with the app, we are here for you.

## Contact us

- **Email:** info@passagesounds.app
- **In the app:** open **Profile → Contact Support** to send us a message, report a problem with a soundscape, or request a new soundscape. Messages sent from inside the app are linked to your account, so we can help you faster.

We aim to reply within a few business days.

## Common questions

**I can't sign in.**
Check that you are connected to the internet and using the email address you registered with. You can reset your password from the sign-in screen with **Forgot password**.

**A soundscape won't play.**
Check your internet connection and try again. If it keeps happening, use **Profile → Contact Support → Report a problem** so we can look into it.

**How do I change or delete my account?**
Open **Profile → Account** to update your name, email, password or profile picture. You can export or permanently delete your account and all of your data from **Profile → Privacy & Data**.

**How is my data handled?**
Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how we use it. Read it at **https://api.passagesounds.app/privacy-policy**.
